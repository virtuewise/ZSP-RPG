# Asset Replacement and Branding Policy

**ZIMBABWE: THE SHADOW PROTOCOL**

## The rule

Nothing that brands, advertises, or is proprietary to
"Commando Army — War Action" ships in this project. Everything the player sees that is
distinctly ours is drawn here.

## What was quarantined

## What we reuse (for now)

Only **generic, non-branded, licensed** extracted assets:

- Environment meshes and generic building/concrete/metal/wood textures (Harare blockouts).
- Generic weapon meshes (AK/FAMAS/MP5 silhouettes) — replaced or retextured before release.
- Humanoid rigs and animation clips — re-rigged or replaced with licensed CGTrader models.
- Mountain and Mirage terrain blockouts.

Anything ambiguous is quarantined first, not shipped first.

## Replacement order

1. All UI chrome, logos, splash, app icon, HUD (done — `Assets/Art/`).
2. Player-facing textures on the Mirage/Mountain kits (Harare signage, decals, posters).
3. Character heads, uniforms, hands, holsters, magazines.
4. Vehicles (kombis, pickups, security patrol car).
5. Audio: weapon reports, radio VO, market ambience, helicopter rotor.
6. Any remaining texture whose filename carries a studio, SDK, or store watermark.

## Legal hygiene

- The APK's ownership remains with its original publisher; we claim no rights in it.
- Extracted assets are used as internal reference until replacement is complete.
- If any extracted asset cannot be cleared, it is deleted from `_Quarantine/` as well.
- No asset ships without a `LICENCE` note in `Assets/Extracted/README.md`.
